Our Story

We didn't want another saree brand with a catalogue. We wanted one with a face.

Every HandLoom saree is worn first by the women who buy it — real fittings, real feedback, real photographs, before anything ships. No retouched drape, no borrowed bodies.

We work loom-to-door with under thirty weaver families, which means smaller runs, longer waits, and a saree that actually looks like the one in the photo.

— The HandLoom Studio

From Fibre to Fold

How a HandLoom saree is made

Five stages, one weaver, six to twenty-one days on the loom depending on the weave.

01

Thread Selection

Mulberry silk and cotton yarn hand-picked and graded for strength and sheen.

02

Dyeing

Small-batch dyeing, matched by eye to each season's palette.

03

Warping

Thousands of warp threads aligned by hand before a single weft is thrown.

04

Weaving

The weaver builds the pallu's motifs thread by thread, over days.

05

Finishing

Edges secured, steam-pressed, and checked against the loom card.
